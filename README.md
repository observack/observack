## Capstone — IDcare

Served as Security Advisor on an R&D project with [IDcare](https://www.idcare.org), Australia and New Zealand's national identity and cyber support service. Researched social engineering and scam tactics across Facebook and Instagram, then built practical account recovery and mitigation guidance for real victims of online fraud.

Working with a national support service meant the output had to be usable by people in distress, not just technically correct — a constraint that shaped how I write security guidance now.
